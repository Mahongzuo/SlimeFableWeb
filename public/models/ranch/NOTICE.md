# 凝露港 · 动物素材署名

下列作品以本地 GLB 和由其渲染的手账图随游戏发布。衍生修改由 SlimeFable Web 制作。

|文件|原作与作者|许可与改动|
|---|---|---|
|capybara、otter、red-panda|[Cozy Animals 3D Models，Gamideo](https://dragofive.itch.io/cozy-animals-3d)|[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)。原作者披露使用 Meshy 生成网格与贴图，并制作程序化骨骼动画。调整吻部、身长、尾部、皮毛色调，平滑轮廓、优化贴图并重导出。|
|hare|[Brown Hare，vinish.dev](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/rabbits-mice-and-squirrels)|[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。减面、优化贴图和动画导出。|
|fox、snow-fox|[Fox，Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox)。模型由 PixelMannen 制作，绑定与动画由 tomkranis 制作，glTF 转换由 Asobo Studio 与 scurest 完成。|原网格为 [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)，绑定、动画及转换为 [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。细分轮廓，新增随头骨运动的眼鼻，重绘赤褐毛色和雪狐冬毛。|
|deer、white-deer、nine-deer|[Deer，vinish.dev](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/animals)|[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。新增侧腹纹理坐标，绘制梅花斑、霜白冬毛和九色纹饰。|
|pheasant|[Rooster，vinish.dev](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/chickens-ducks-and-geese)|[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。采用雉科家鸡的骨骼和动作，重塑冠羽、延长尾羽、重绘披肩与腹羽并减面。|
|crane|[White Stork，vinish.dev](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/water-birds)|[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。以白鹳的涉禽步态为基础调整颈部轮廓，重绘颈羽、红顶、飞羽和腿色并减面。|
